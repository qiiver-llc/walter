Optional: admin password for the laptop

Lets you log in to the laptop (user walter, e.g. over SSH at walter.local) to fix things. Leave empty to skip; you can't set it later from here.

Optional: give Walter full admin rights

Walter can then install software, change system settings and fix the laptop himself, without asking for a password. The catch: Walter reads web pages and email, so a malicious page or message could try to trick him into doing something harmful, and with admin rights the damage isn't limited to his own files. Only tick this on a laptop used just for Walter, keep backups of anything that matters, and know you can take it back at any time by deleting /etc/sudoers.d/90-walter.
